# Image Upload Guide

Your website is now set up to display images. Follow these steps to add your pictures:

## Image Locations

All images should be placed in the `/images` folder:
```
personal-blog/
  images/
    profile.jpg          (Your profile picture - shows in top right of header)
    activity-1.jpg       (Activity/project photo)
    activity-2.jpg       (Activity/project photo)
    activity-3.jpg       (Activity/project photo)
    activity-4.jpg       (Activity/project photo)
    activity-5.jpg       (Activity/project photo)
    activity-6.jpg       (Activity/project photo)
```

## Image Requirements

- **Format**: JPG, PNG, or WebP
- **Profile Picture**: Square format (e.g., 500x500px) - displays as 100px circular in top right of header
- **Activity Photos**: Landscape or square (e.g., 400x300px or 400x400px) - displays in gallery grid
- **File Size**: Optimize to under 500KB each for faster loading
- **Quality**: Use web-optimized images (72-96 DPI)

## How to Add Images

1. Save your images in the `/images` folder with these exact filenames:
   - `profile.jpg` - Your profile/headshot
   - `activity-1.jpg` through `activity-6.jpg` - Your activities, projects, or any photos you want to showcase

2. The images will automatically appear on your website:
   - Profile picture: Top right corner of the header
   - Activity photos: Gallery grid in the "About Me" section with hover effects

## Features

✅ **Profile Picture**: Circular with blue border and glow effect - displays at 100px on desktop, 80px on mobile

✅ **Activity Gallery**: Responsive grid that adapts to screen size with smooth hover zoom effect

✅ **WhatsApp Button**: Green floating button in bottom right corner
   - Click to open welcome modal
   - Shows your name and asks if they want to chat
   - Links to your WhatsApp: 0721894141

## Tips

- Use high-quality photos that represent your work and personality
- Make sure profile picture has good lighting and a clear view of your face
- Activity photos should showcase real projects, events, or accomplishments
- Keep images consistent in style and quality
- Consider using tools like:
  - Canva (edit/resize images)
  - TinyPNG (compress file size)
  - Pixlr (quick edits)

## File Naming Important!

Make sure filenames match exactly:
- ✅ `profile.jpg` (correct)
- ❌ `Profile.jpg` (wrong - case matters)
- ❌ `profile.JPG` (may not work on all systems)
- ❌ `my-profile-pic.jpg` (wrong filename)

Once you upload your images to the `/images` folder, they will appear automatically on your website!
