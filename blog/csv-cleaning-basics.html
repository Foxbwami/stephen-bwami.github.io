CSV files are one of the most common formats used for storing and sharing data. However, raw CSV data is often messy and unreliable.

Common issues include missing values, duplicate rows, inconsistent formats, and unclear column names. Cleaning data ensures accuracy and usability.

As a data analyst, my first step is always to understand the structure of the data before applying any analysis.

Clean data leads to better insights, fewer errors, and more confident decisions.
