Common CSV Data Mistakes and How to Fix Them

April 2026

Many CSV files look correct at first glance but contain hidden problems that can distort analysis results.

Common issues include inconsistent date formats, empty cells, duplicate records, and incorrect data types that break calculations or lead to misleading insights.

Fixing these problems requires careful inspection, validation, and consistency checks before analysis begins.

By identifying and correcting these mistakes early, you improve data quality and increase confidence in your conclusions.
