How I Clean CSV Files for Data Analysis

March 2026

Before any analysis begins, I focus on cleaning the dataset. CSV files often contain missing values, duplicates, and inconsistent formats.

My first step is to inspect column names and data types. Clear and consistent naming makes the dataset easier to understand and maintain.

Next, I handle missing values by either filling them with defaults or removing rows when necessary. The decision depends on the dataset’s purpose.

Finally, I remove duplicates and standardize formats such as dates and numbers. Clean data ensures reliable analysis and trustworthy results.

CSV cleaning is not optional. It is the foundation of good data work.
