Why Data Cleaning Matters More Than Analysis

February 2026 · By Stephen Bwami

Many people think data analysis is about charts, models, and formulas. In reality, much of the work happens before any analysis begins.

Raw data is often incomplete, duplicated, or inconsistent. If these problems are ignored, the final results can become misleading.

Data cleaning helps ensure that values are accurate, formats are consistent, and errors are handled. This step protects decisions from being based on false information.

In practice, analysts often spend significant time preparing data. This is not a weakness — it is what makes insights more trustworthy.

Clean data leads to confidence. Confidence supports better decisions.
