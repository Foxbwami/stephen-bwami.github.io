01 / THE JOURNEY

MY STORY

01 — THE FOUNDATION

Technology caught my attention. Building things gave it direction.

I’m pursuing a Bachelor of Business Information Technology at the Technical University of Mombasa. The course sits at an intersection I find exciting: business, information and technology. It has helped shape the way I look at problems — not only asking how a system works, but also who it serves, what information it handles and whether it makes a task easier.

My interest goes beyond learning concepts for examinations. I want to understand how those concepts behave in real applications. That means writing code, testing ideas, getting things wrong, debugging, improving the design and trying again. Each project becomes another opportunity to turn classroom knowledge and curiosity into practical experience.

I’m still growing, and I’m comfortable saying that. I see the journey as a continuous process of learning, building, receiving feedback and becoming better at the work.
